Written on 11 October 2026 with an AI writing assistant (not Gemini, the model under evaluation). The same assistant designed the frame-ack contradiction-detection method, so this series is NOT BLIND: report it as a second synthetic series written after the method's prompts were frozen (frames-v1, ack-v1), not as a blind held-out test. The ground truth (decisions.csv, relations.csv, non_decisions.csv) was fixed together with the transcripts, before the system was run on them. To limit the author's bias it deliberately contains hard cases: a contradiction whose earlier option is mentioned in between without any change being agreed (G16 vs G3), a revision acknowledged only indirectly (G7 vs G2), changes agreed in one meeting and only re-confirmed later (G13, G20 vs G2), and look-alike decisions on different subjects (G11 vs G1/G17, G14 vs G2). Domain: an outpatient booking app for a hospital's clinics, used by no other evaluation series or prompt. The transcripts are LLM-written and cleaner than real meetings; the team should review them for realism before publication.
