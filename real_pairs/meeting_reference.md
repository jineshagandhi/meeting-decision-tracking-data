## ES2004

### Meeting 1 - ES2004a (ES2004 part 1)

> **Gold summary (QMSum):** Project Manager introduced a new remote control project. The team started to know each other by drawing favourite animals. Project Manager proposed to price each remote control at 25 Euros, considering the 12.5-Euro production cost. The market range would be international and over all age groups. After the financial targets, the team discussed the remote design. Despite the "one-for-all" style, the remote was still supposed to be user-friendly. Standard features such as sorting through channels and adjusting volume would not lose their priority. The team, therefore, agreed on a style of basic buttons with a self-intuitive screen menu. Silver lightweight plastic was temporarily decided to be the material, for it is easily moulded and cost-saving.


**Decisions extracted by the system:**

- (none)

### Meeting 2 - ES2004b (ES2004 part 2)

> **Gold summary (QMSum):** The team mainly made some adjustments to the remote control design to meet the new project requirements. The second meeting started with Project Manager who briefly went over the last meeting. Industrial Designer hoped to minimize the size of the battery. The team thus decided on a rechargeable one. User Interface desired to simplify the remote control style, cutting down unnecessary functions. According to the market research presented by Marketing, the team agreed on a trendy and user-friendly flip-top design. An alarm would be incorporated for detection in case the remote control was lost in the house.


**Decisions extracted by the system:**

- D1: Exclude teletext functionality from the remote control design.
- D2: Limit the remote control's primary focus to TV (and basic video features) rather than a universal controller for all video equipment.
- D3: Use rechargeable batteries with a docking station for the remote control.
- D4: Maintain a standardized internal engineering design across all aesthetic variations of the product.

### Meeting 3 - ES2004c (ES2004 part 3)

> **Gold summary (QMSum):** After a brief summary of the last meeting, the team discussed the conceptual design for new remote control, which covered the properties, materials, user interface and trend watching. Marketing shared feedback about the design of the device. Industrial Designer gave a presentation on the design and availability of actual components, whereas User Interface led the discussion about customer needs and feasibility on design and competitors' study. A final decision was reached regarding the design of the remote control such as using kinetic charging and a rubber cover.


**Decisions extracted by the system:**

- D5: Adopt kinetic charging using a watch-style battery as the power source for the remote control.
- D6: Construct the main body out of a plastic casing with a removable, spongy rubber outer cover.
- D7: Use fruit and vegetable color themes for the rubber outer covers.
- D8: Implement a flip-style design featuring a black-and-white LCD display on the top inner panel and rubber anti-RSI buttons on the bottom panel.
- D9: Position standard, frequently used controls (channel and volume) on the lower panel for thumb operation.

### Meeting 4 - ES2004d (ES2004 part 4)

> **Gold summary (QMSum):** The team mainly planned the remote control project down to the last detail in the final meeting. Industrial Designer presented the prototype as a clam-shell design with a flip-top LCD touch screen and fully closed flat buttons at the bottom. There would be a wide choice of colours and patterns due to customization. Inside the remote control, there would be a standard chip and a kinetic battery. The team was under the budget at present. Once the budget increased, however, the voice recognition would be incorporated. The remote control's fashion style and location function were most highly rated among the team members, but the minimization of repetitive strain injury was not satisfying enough. All members gave their sincere feedback on teamwork and leadership.


**Decisions extracted by the system:**

- D10: Proceed with the current remote control prototype design without making further modifications.
- D11: Remove the voice recognition feature from the base product model to stay within the €12.50 production budget, while offering it as a recommendation for an upgraded or business-class version.

## ES2011

### Meeting 1 - ES2011a (ES2011 part 1)

> **Gold summary (QMSum):** Project Manager invites teammates to brainstorm animal characteristics on remote control function and suggest a reflection on user experience to improve technical function design. Price goal is 25 Euro, profit aim is fifty million Euro, and sales goal is four million. To summarize, the function design will be user-friendly and practical, in which the technical function will be portable phone based, hand-sized, light with limited but all-functioned buttons.


**Decisions extracted by the system:**

- (none)

### Meeting 2 - ES2011b (ES2011 part 2)

> **Gold summary (QMSum):** The meeting started by discussing the functional design of the remote control. Marketing gave the study on user needs and target groups. User Interface gave the presentation on technical effect design and Industrial Designer gave the presentation on working design. Then Project Manager initiated new project requirements brainstorming. The group had agreed on the on-call button design, DVD capability button design, power button design, and denied speech recognition design as well as mouse pad design. They would further discuss the function switch button and its compatibility in the next meeting.


**Decisions extracted by the system:**

- D12: Agreed to target teenagers and young professionals as the primary user group.
- D13: Approved the inclusion of a two-part locator/finder system, which consists of a radio-frequency transmitter base stuck to the TV and a buzzer output on the remote control itself.
- D14: Rejected the inclusion of speech recognition and an on-remote screen due to programming complexity, potential ambient noise interference, and budget constraints.
- D15: Excluded teletext functionality from the remote control design as per updated project requirements.

### Meeting 3 - ES2011c (ES2011 part 3)

> **Gold summary (QMSum):** This meeting was mainly about conceptual design. Industrial Designer, Marketing and User Interface gave presentations mainly on component design, current trend and interface design respectively, based on their research on the website incorporating the ideas from last meeting. A lot of discussions was centred around the fruit and vegetable theme, which was the current marketing trend. In the meanwhile, they had some discussions on physical attributes and the use of energy. They agreed that the screen was not necessary, and TV could be used as the screen instead. In addition, Kryptonite became their preferred energy.


**Decisions extracted by the system:**

- D16: Display the user interface menu on the TV screen instead of using an expensive on-device screen on the remote itself.
- D17: Use an iPod-style scroll wheel as the primary control mechanism for the remote control.
- D18: Use batteries as the power source for the remote control.

### Meeting 4 - ES2011d (ES2011 part 4)

> **Gold summary (QMSum):** Project Manager initiated the meeting topic on the detailed design. Specifically, the prototype presentation was given by Industrial Designer, and calculation on each evaluation criteria was led by Marketing. Next, group members discussed changing aspects of the remote. Finally, they decided on changing the shape, instead of batteries. Then, Project Manager had a discussion on the process of the project with other teammates, including room for creativity, leadership, and convenience of materials. The group decided to write a final report in the end.


**Decisions extracted by the system:**

- D19: The team agreed to change the design of the remote control from distinct, physical fruit shapes to a uniform, ergonomic shape, using different colors or printed fruit patterns to represent the various fruits.
